# Ice

A pixel-art puzzle: on ice, the penguin cannot stop. It slides to the next rock or the edge. Get it to the flag.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## Daily level and editor (new in 1.2.0)

A new level every day, the same for everyone, with a day streak. The editor lets you place rocks, snow, the penguin and the flag, shows the shortest path right away, lets you try your level, keep it and share it with a code.

## Controls

- **Arrows**: slide · **Backspace** or **U**: undo · **R**: restart · **H**: hint · **P**: pause.
- **Mouse**: click the side to slide towards.
- **Gamepad**: d-pad or stick slides, **X** undoes, **Y** gives a hint, **LB** restarts.

## New after level 60

- **Cracked ice** (from level 61): you can slide over it once; it breaks when you leave it and becomes water, which then stops you like a rock.
- **Snow piles** (from 66): slide into one to push it; it slides in turn to an obstacle and you stay behind.
- **Teleporters** (from 71): go into one, come out of the other, keeping your direction.
- **Full screen**: the button of the same name; Escape to leave it.

## How it works

- **Snow** stops the penguin dead; so does the **flag**.
- **Three stars** for the shortest path, two if you need two moves more.
- A **hint** shows the right move, but the level is then worth one star.
- 80 levels, each one checked solvable.

## Data and sync

Levels and stars are saved by NeoDex and synced between your PCs if sync is on.
